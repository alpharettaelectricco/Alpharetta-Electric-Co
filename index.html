Full Step-Up: More than a setup

Every guitar we offer is inspected for neck geometry, fret condition, action, tuning stability, and electronic performance. Any adjustments or corrective work needed to unlock its full feel — from fret polishing to nut refinement or spot-leveling — are performed with intention and care. This is more than a simple setup. It’s a full "step-up" to ensure the playability matches the potential of the instrument.

Our vision is simple: if you love playing this guitar, you’ll love playing the guitar.

After a full Alpharetta Electric Co. Step-Up, though, this thing plays better than it did even when it was a new guitar. The neck is straight, the frets are clean, and the intonation is spot on.
