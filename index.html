It’s a full "step-up" to ensure the playability matches the potential of the instrument.

Our vision is simple: if you love playing this guitar, you’ll love playing the guitar.

Fretwork: Passed & Cleaned
Inspected for tuning stability

This guitar is the embodiment of my business model. I bought this one from a young man on Facebook Marketplace. He told me it had sat unplayed for several years, and I could totally see why. The input jack was loose and wiggling. It was dusty and grimy. The strings were lined with what I can only assume was tetanus. And the action... oh, the action. It was so high that it was unplayable. I don't blame him for giving up on that guitar. But I hate the idea that he might have given up on the guitar when he did.

After a full Alpharetta Electric Co. Step-Up, though, this thing plays better than it did even when it was a new guitar. The neck is straight, the frets are clean, and the intonation is spot on. It's a great example of how a little bit of love and care can turn a neglected guitar into something special. In fact, this guitar in particular was very special. It's unusual to get an entry-level instrument dialed in at .005" relief with 3/64" action on the treble side and 4/64" action on the bass side. But this neck is so straight, and these factory frets are so good that it plays as good as the $3,500 Iconic Guitars S-Style I have in the shop. It's a testament to the fact that you don't need to spend a lot of money to get a great playing guitar. You just need to know how to step it up properly.

If you're turned off by a Yamaha guitar, you shouldn't be. There is a reason almost every guitar teacher and tech recommends Yamaha. While other budget brands rely on flash, Yamaha focuses on relentless consistency. They are built like tanks, meaning the neck geometry and fretwork are incredibly stable right out of the factory. The tradeoff with a brand-new beginner model is that the factory setup is usually generic—designed to safely survive a shipping container, not to feel like a custom shop instrument. That’s where our Step-Up process comes in. We’ve taken an already rock-solid platform and dialed in the neck relief, refined the nut slots, and polished the frets with precision. Don’t buy a generic factory box online. This Yamaha has been vetted, sorted, and meticulously brought up to code to ensure it is an absolute joy to play from day one.
