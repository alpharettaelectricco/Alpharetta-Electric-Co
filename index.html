Full Step-Up: More than a setup

Every guitar we offer is inspected for neck geometry, fret condition, action, tuning stability, and electronic performance. Any adjustments or corrective work needed to unlock its full feel — from fret polishing to nut refinement or spot-leveling — are performed with intention and care. This is more than a simple setup. It’s a full "step-up" to ensure the playability matches the potential of the instrument.

Our vision is simple: if you love playing this guitar, you’ll love playing the guitar.

Firefly guitars are built for the player who wants the aesthetic of a vintage or boutique instrument and modern high-end appointments—like a roasted maple neck and stainless steel frets—without the boutique investment. We will only say this one looks like a boutique guitar because the distressed white-over-sunburst is somewhat of a graphic finish rather than the result of multiple layers of hand-sprayed nitrocellulose lacquer that's been hand-aged by a master luthier. It’s not what I’d call "pretty" in a traditional sense, but it’s undeniably cool. The neck has the appearance of roasted maple, but it's not the premium hardwood you'd find on a $3,000 guitar. And while we have certainly imrpoved the shape and condition of those frets A LOT, they are not the stainless steel frets you’d find on a boutique instrument. That being said, this Firefly S-Style is a great-looking guitar with a solid build and a great sound. The tradeoff with these Firefly guitars is their reputation for being rough out of the box. That is where our Step-Up process comes in. We’ve handled the technical heavy lifting—leveling the frets, refining the nut, and dialing in the action—to ensure the playability finally matches the look. Don’t risk the "online dud" lottery. This specific guitar has been vetted, sorted, and brought up to code. It’s ready to play the moment you pick it up.
